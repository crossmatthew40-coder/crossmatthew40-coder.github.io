About High Style

Hospitality,
properly seen.

“Great hospitality photography should make someone want to taste it, book it or be there.”

High Style Creative is a hospitality-focused photography studio led by Matt Cross.

The work spans food, drink, hotels, restaurants, people, service and behind-the-scenes storytelling. The approach is commercial without feeling over-produced: authentic enough for guests, polished enough for the brand.

High Style works across the UK, creating imagery for hospitality teams that needs to perform across websites, menus, social media, campaigns, internal specifications and launches.
