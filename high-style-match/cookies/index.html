Cookie & Storage Policy

What High Style Match stores on your device, why it is needed, and how to control optional analytics.

Last updated 9 September 2026

Current default

Optional analytics is off by default. Essential browser storage is used only for functions the user requests, such as authentication, project state, privacy choices and security.

1. What counts as storage?

Cookie rules can also apply to localStorage, sessionStorage and similar device storage. High Style Match treats these technologies as part of its cookie/storage controls.

2. Essential storage

  • Authentication/session state for signed-in accounts.
  • Project and workflow state needed to reopen work.
  • Offline queues and file references needed for local-first operation.
  • Your cookie/storage preference and policy acknowledgement.
  • Security and fraud-prevention state where necessary.

These are used only where necessary to provide the service you requested or to remember your privacy choice.

3. Optional analytics

Optional product analytics requires an affirmative choice where consent is required. The public build does not enable advertising cookies or behavioural advertising. If Global Privacy Control is enabled in your browser, High Style Match keeps optional analytics disabled.

4. Third parties

Core service connections may be made to GitHub Pages, Supabase and software-delivery infrastructure needed to run the app. High Style Match does not require social-media tracking pixels or third-party advertising embeds for its core workflow.

5. Change your choice

Use the control in the app or legal footer. You can also clear site storage in your browser.

6. UK rules

High Style Match's controls are designed around the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and UK GDPR consent/transparency requirements. Essential storage is separated from optional analytics choices.
