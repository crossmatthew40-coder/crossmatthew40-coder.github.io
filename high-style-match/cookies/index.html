Cookie & local storage policy

High Style Match currently uses essential browser storage for the local photography workflow. Advertising and analytics cookies are not enabled on the current public build.

If analytics, advertising or other non-essential technologies are introduced later, they must remain disabled until the appropriate consent choice is made.

Storage used now

StoragePurposeType
Project dataKeeps local shoots, workflow metadata and preferences available on this device.Essential local storage / IndexedDB
Session stateKeeps temporary navigation, sign-in bypass and delivery-session state for the current browser session.Essential session storage
Cookie notice choiceRemembers that you have seen the essential-storage notice.Essential local storage

Tracking

The current public build does not intentionally load advertising pixels or analytics trackers. Third-party services used later for authentication, payments or delivery may set their own essential cookies when those services are enabled.

Your controls

You can clear local site data through your browser settings. Clearing it may remove locally stored High Style Match projects or preferences that have not been backed up elsewhere.
