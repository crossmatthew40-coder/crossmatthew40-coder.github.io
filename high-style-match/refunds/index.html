Refund & Cancellation Policy

How refunds, subscription cancellation, duplicate charges and UK statutory cancellation rights are handled.

Last updated 9 September 2026

Statutory rights come first

This policy does not reduce rights you may have under UK consumer law. If a service is faulty or not supplied with reasonable care and skill, legal remedies may apply regardless of this policy.

1. Free/public builds

If no payment was taken for your use of a High Style Match build, there is no purchase price to refund.

2. Paid subscriptions

Where paid subscriptions are offered, you can cancel future renewal through the billing controls provided. Cancellation normally stops future charges rather than deleting your projects immediately.

3. UK consumer cancellation period

If you buy online as a consumer, a statutory cancellation period may apply. If you expressly ask us to start supplying a service during that period, a proportionate charge may be due for service already supplied. For digital content made available immediately, the right to cancel can be affected where you expressly consent to immediate supply and acknowledge the consequence required by law.

4. Duplicate, mistaken or unauthorised charges

Contact us promptly through the Business & contact route. We will investigate duplicate charges, billing errors and credible unauthorised-use reports.

5. Faults

If a paid service materially fails to work as described, contact us with the affected account, date and problem. We may repair the service, provide an appropriate alternative, credit the account or refund where required by law or reasonably appropriate.

6. Business customers

Where you buy primarily for business purposes, cancellation/refund rights may differ from consumer rights and can be governed by the specific order, quote or subscription agreement.
