Refunds & cancellation

High Style Match is not currently taking subscription payments on the public build. No payment can be charged from the current subscription page.

Before paid plans launch

Paid checkout will only be enabled after the billing provider, account system and subscription controls have been configured and tested. The checkout flow will show the price, billing frequency, trial terms if any, renewal terms and cancellation method before purchase.

Cancellation

When subscriptions are enabled, users will be able to cancel future renewal through the billing controls. Cancellation will stop future renewal; access for any already-paid period will be explained clearly in the checkout terms.

Refund requests

Any refund rights required by applicable law will not be restricted by this policy. For discretionary refund requests, contact support@highstylegroup.co.uk with the account email and payment details once billing is live.

Digital services and trials

If a free trial or immediate digital-service access is offered, the checkout flow will explain the relevant trial and cancellation conditions before the user confirms payment.
