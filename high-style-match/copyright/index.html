Copyright & Image Rights

Who owns uploaded photographs, what permission High Style Match needs, and what users must confirm before processing files.

Last updated 9 September 2026

Your images remain yours

High Style Match does not claim ownership of photographs, shot lists or client content you supply. You must have permission to process and share them.

1. User content

You retain copyright and other rights in your content. The service receives only the limited licence needed to perform functions you request, such as analysing, previewing, matching, renaming, storing, reviewing and exporting files.

2. Rights confirmation

Upload and delivery workflows include a rights confirmation. Do not upload photographs, logos, briefs or other material that you do not own or have authority to process.

3. People in photographs

Copyright permission and privacy/model-release obligations are separate. You are responsible for obtaining any permissions or releases required for your intended use and for complying with client contracts.

4. High Style Match assets

The High Style Match interface, brand marks, original code, documentation and product graphics are protected by applicable intellectual-property rights. Using the app does not transfer ownership of those assets.

5. Copyright concerns

If you believe content delivered through a High Style Match project infringes your rights, use the contact route on the Business & contact page with enough information to identify the material and your rights.
