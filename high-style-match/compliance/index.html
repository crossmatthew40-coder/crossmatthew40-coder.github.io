Compliance engineering checklist

This maps the safeguards implemented in High Style Match to the checklist supplied. It is an engineering/audit record, not a legal certification.

Checklist

✅ Colour contrastCore palette and focus states use high-contrast combinations; page-level checking remains part of release QA.
✅ Alt text on imagesMissing useful alt text is filled for logos and previews; decorative graphics can stay hidden.
✅ Refund policyRefund & Cancellation Policy is published and preserves statutory consumer rights.
✅ Privacy policyPrivacy Notice covers data categories, purposes, legal bases, providers, retention, rights and automated analysis.
✅ AccessibilitySkip links, focus rings, keyboard activation, reduced motion, form labels and accessibility statement are included.
✅ Remove fake reviewsKnown demo/fake-review elements are removed by runtime and fabricated testimonials are prohibited.
✅ T&Cs pageTerms cover image rights, AI limitations, backups, accounts, subscriptions, liability and governing law.
✅ Check 3rd-party embedsIframes are labelled/lazy-loaded and a runtime audit reports third-party resources.
✅ Copyright on imagesPhoto import/delivery can require rights confirmation and a copyright policy is published.
✅ Cookies policyEssential storage and optional analytics are documented separately.
✅ Check trackingKnown tracking hosts are audited; optional analytics is off by default and advertising tracking is not part of the core app.
✅ Form consentAccount creation requires Terms acceptance and Privacy acknowledgement; signed-in consent records can be persisted.
✅ Check local lawsPolicies are written for a UK-operated service and reference UK GDPR, DPA 2018, PECR and applicable consumer rules.
✅ Clear button labelsRuntime adds accessible names to otherwise unlabeled controls.
✅ Cookie consentFirst-use choices separate essential storage from optional analytics and honour Global Privacy Control.
✅ Real business detailsBusiness page identifies product, brand, operating brand, UK jurisdiction and a real contact route.
✅ Only collect necessary dataLocal-first processing, optional cloud originals and limited operational logging reduce collection.
✅ Keyboard-friendly formsInputs are named, custom controls are keyboard-activatable and focus states are visible.
✅ Remove unsupported claimsAI features are described as assistive suggestions and unverifiable guarantees/fabricated reviews are not allowed.
