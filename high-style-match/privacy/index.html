Privacy Policy

High Style Match · High Style Group

This page is a practical product-policy draft and should be reviewed for your final business entity, contact details and legal obligations before public commercial launch.

What we collect

High Style Match may process account details such as name and email address, client and project information, shot-list metadata, image previews selected for review, customer comments and approvals, delivery records, billing status, device/browser information and technical error logs.

Photography files

The product is designed so original RAW files can remain on the photographer's device during a tethered shoot. Where cloud review is enabled, optimized previews or delivery files may be uploaded to private storage. Access is controlled by authenticated project permissions.

Why we use information

  • To provide accounts, project access and cloud synchronization.
  • To allow photographers and customers to review, approve and deliver work.
  • To keep project history, backups and audit records.
  • To diagnose failures, protect the service and improve reliability.
  • To manage subscriptions and service communications where enabled.

Who can see project data

Photographers can access projects they own. Customers are intended to see only projects explicitly shared with their account. High Style Match uses project-level authorization and database Row Level Security when cloud accounts are enabled.

Service providers

The production version may use infrastructure providers such as Supabase for authentication/database/storage, Stripe for billing, and an email provider for transactional messages. Their own privacy terms apply to processing they perform on behalf of the service.

Retention and deletion

Project and account information is retained while needed to provide the service or meet legal/business requirements. Account holders should be provided with controls to request an export or deletion of their personal data. Backup copies may take a reasonable period to expire.

Security

Private server credentials are not stored in browser code. Customer project access should not be enabled until authentication, private storage and Row Level Security have been configured and tested.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict or delete personal information, and to complain to the relevant data-protection authority.
