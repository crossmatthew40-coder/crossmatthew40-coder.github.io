Privacy Notice

How High Style Match handles account, project, image and service data, including UK GDPR transparency information.

Last updated 9 September 2026

Plain-language summary

High Style Match is designed to minimise data collection. The browser/desktop workflow can process photographs locally. Cloud features are optional and, when used, store only the data needed to provide account, project, review, backup and delivery features.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

High Style Match is a High Style Group product operated in the United Kingdom. The current operator and contact route are listed on the Business & contact page. That operator acts as controller for account, billing, support and service administration data. Where a photographer uploads client photographs or information, the photographer may also be a controller for that content.

2. Information we may process

  • Account data such as name/studio name, email, authentication identifiers and role.
  • Project and shot-list information, filenames, project notes, client names and shoot dates.
  • Photograph metadata, thumbnails/previews and originals only where you intentionally use a cloud backup, review or delivery feature.
  • Client review decisions, comments, approvals and invitations.
  • Billing/subscription identifiers if paid billing is enabled; payment card details are handled by the payment provider, not stored directly by High Style Match.
  • Operational logs needed for security, troubleshooting and service reliability.
  • Consent/acknowledgement records for Terms, privacy notices, image rights and optional analytics choices.

3. Why we use it and legal bases

  • Contract: to create accounts, provide project workflows, process files you select, save project state, provide reviews and deliver requested services.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure, maintain, troubleshoot and improve the service in ways that do not override your rights.
  • Legal obligation: where records are required for tax, consumer, fraud-prevention or regulatory obligations.
  • Consent: for optional analytics or marketing where consent is required. Optional analytics is off by default.

4. Smart Cull and automated analysis

Smart Cull is decision-support software. It can rank or flag images using technical and visual signals, but its suggestions do not make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on people. Photographers remain in control and can override results.

5. Where data is processed

The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Authenticated cloud features use Supabase for authentication, database and private storage. The app may load the Supabase client library through a software CDN. Payment processing may use Stripe if billing is enabled. These providers may receive technical connection data such as IP address and browser/device information under their own privacy terms.

No third-party social-media embeds or advertising trackers are required by the core High Style Match workflow.

6. How long we keep information

Project/account content is kept while needed to provide the service or until you delete it, subject to backups and legal requirements. Operational error logs are designed for short retention (normally no more than 90 days) and service audit records for longer security/accountability retention (normally no more than 365 days), unless an incident or legal requirement requires longer.

7. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to processing, receive portable data and withdraw consent. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). Use the contact route on the Business & contact page to exercise a right.

8. Data minimisation & security

We design features to collect only information needed for the requested function. Original photographs remain local unless you deliberately enable a feature that uploads them. Private cloud storage uses authenticated access controls. High Style Match does not use uploaded client photographs to train public AI models in the current service.

9. Children

High Style Match is a professional photography workflow and is not directed at children. Accounts are intended for people who can enter into the relevant contract in their jurisdiction.
