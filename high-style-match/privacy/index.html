Privacy Policy

High Style Match · High Style Group

The current public build is primarily local-first. Cloud accounts, customer cloud projects and paid billing are disabled until their external services are configured and tested.

What we collect now

When you use the local web app, project information, shot-list metadata, preferences and selected photography files may be stored locally in your browser using local storage, session storage or IndexedDB. This data stays on the device unless you deliberately use a feature that sends information to a connected external service.

Photography files

Original photography files can remain on the photographer's device during local workflows. Where delivery or cloud review is later enabled, files or optimized previews may be uploaded only after the user starts that feature.

Accounts and cloud services

Authentication, shared customer projects, cloud synchronization and paid billing are not active on the current public build. When enabled, this policy will be updated with the exact providers, purposes, retention periods and user controls that apply.

Why information is used

  • To provide the local photography workflow and remember project state.
  • To process files and metadata the user selects.
  • To provide delivery, accounts or collaboration when those services are intentionally enabled.
  • To protect the service and diagnose technical problems where monitoring is enabled.

Cookies and tracking

The current public build does not intentionally use advertising or analytics trackers. Essential browser storage is used for the app itself. See the Cookie & local storage policy.

Third parties

Future production features may use service providers for authentication, storage, payments, email or delivery. Those services will only be described as active once actually connected. Third-party providers process data under their own terms and applicable agreements.

Retention and deletion

Local project data remains on the device until you remove it, clear browser storage, or the browser removes it. If cloud accounts are enabled later, account export and deletion controls should be available before real customer data is invited into the service.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and complaints to the relevant data-protection authority. Mandatory rights are not intended to be limited by this policy.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@highstylegroup.co.uk. Product and business information is available on the Legal & business information page.
